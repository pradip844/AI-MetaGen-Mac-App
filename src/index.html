📉 Reducing Rejection Chance

1. Image Quality (Most Common)

Ensure your file meets these technical standards:

  • Resolution: Minimum 4 Megapixels (e.g., 2500x1600px).
  • Focus & Sharpness: Subject must be perfectly sharp (check at 100% zoom).
  • Noise/Artifacts: Avoid grainy shadows or JPEG compression artifacts.
  • Lighting: Avoid overexposure (blown highlights) or underexposure.

2. Intellectual Property

Critical: Never include visible logos, trademarks, or protected designs.
  • Remove logos from clothes, devices, or backgrounds.
  • Avoid recognizable designs (e.g., modern cars, specific buildings).
  • Model Release: Required for ANY recognizable person.
  • Property Release: Required for recognizable private property/art.

3. Metadata & Relevance

Tip: Use our AI Generator to create accurate, relevant metadata.
  • Title: Must describe the image accurately (don't spam keywords).
  • Keywords: Relevant terms only. Misleading keywords lead to rejection.
  • Spam: Do not repeat words in the title excessively.

4. AI-Generated Content

  • Labeling: Almost all platforms REQUIRE you to label AI content as "Generative AI" or "AI Generated".
  • Anatomy: Check for AI glitches (extra fingers, weird eyes, floating objects).
  • Text: Remove any nonsense/gibberish text generated by AI.
